PUBLIC SECTOR REQUEST FOR PROPOSAL — REF. EMET-DA-2026-CLOUD-AI
Contracting Authority: European Ministry of Ecological Transition / Digital Agency
Subject: Migration to a Sovereign Low-Carbon Cloud & AI Data Platform

1. STRATEGIC CONTEXT & OBJECTIVES
The European Ministry of Ecological Transition and its Digital Agency are modernizing their legacy data and analytics infrastructure currently hosted on aging on-premises datacenters. The objective is to deploy a unified, sovereign Cloud and AI platform capable of processing national carbon emissions, biodiversity, and water-resilience telemetry.

2. TECHNICAL & ARCHITECTURAL SCOPE
- Migration of 42 TB of structured and unstructured datasets to a sovereign European Cloud architecture (SecNumCloud or equivalent EU sovereignty guarantees).
- Deployment of real-time DataOps pipelines, a governed Lakehouse, and a generative AI decision-support layer for public policy analysts.
- Strict integration with ministerial identity providers (SSO / OIDC) and customer-managed encryption keys (CMEK).
- Availability target: guaranteed 99.95% production SLA, multi-zone disaster recovery with RPO < 15 minutes and RTO < 1 hour.

3. ESG, CSRD & GREEN IT REQUIREMENTS (ELIMINATORY WEIGHTING: 30%)
- Mandatory hosting in France within a certified low-carbon Cloud region (e.g., GCP europe-west9 Paris, powered predominantly by decarbonized electricity).
- Full compliance with the EU CSRD (Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive): granular Scope 2 & Scope 3 digital carbon footprint tracking across AI workloads and storage.
- Software eco-design (RGESN / Green IT standards), continuous FinOps optimization, and automated shutdown of non-production environments outside business hours.
- Contractual commitment to a -40% reduction in energy intensity compared to the legacy datacenter baseline.

4. SECURITY, PRIVACY & GDPR COMPLIANCE
- Automated PII detection and masking (Cloud DLP) of citizen and civil-servant records prior to any AI model indexing or inference.
- Exclusive residency of operational data and immutable audit logs within French territory.

5. COMMERCIAL TERMS & TIMELINE
- Ceiling budget: €1.8M excl. VAT over 24 months (Build: €1.1M, Managed Run & FinOps: €0.7M).
- Proposal submission deadline: December 15, 2026 at 12:00 CET.
- Target kickoff for Discovery & Landing Zone phase: January 2027.
